Play the ads

Five HTML5 playables, one for each video. Two guided swipes, then the player makes the last move alone. A miss gives one retry, a win unlocks a bonus level, then the end card.

1.1 MB single HTML each, built for AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource (MRAID), Google Ads, Meta and Mintegral. Same rules as the game: the JS port is checked against the Unity build on 9,900 moves.

Video ads

Real gameplay, a mistake on the last move, then the end card. 9:16, 1080×1920, 16–18 seconds, with sound.

The game

Four rules. Land cats on the fish. Water sends a cat to the bench. Dogs chase bones and bite. Keep at least as many cats as bowls.

Content

  • 141 levels in 8 chapters: Yard to Castle
  • 140 daily puzzles with weekly rules
  • Every level checked by our BFS solver
  • Levels 142–241 generated and checked

Meta & retention

  • 48 named cats in 8 sets, play your favourite
